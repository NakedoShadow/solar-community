# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Solar Community exists to make communities more autonomous, not to reproduce within itself the power imbalances it seeks to undo elsewhere. The same non-harm principle that governs the project's technical design — electrical safety first, see [docs/safety/](docs/safety/README.en.md) — applies to the community building it: we pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

**Behavior that contributes to a healthy environment:**
- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility for mistakes, apologizing to those affected, and learning from the experience
- Focusing on what's best not just for oneself individually, but for the community as a whole and the project's goal

**Unacceptable behavior:**
- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Publicly encouraging or downplaying the risk of an electrical installation not validated by a professional — see [docs/safety/](docs/safety/README.en.md)
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies across all project spaces (issues, pull requests, GitHub discussions) and whenever someone is officially representing the project in a public space.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainer at **ADA_Shadow@proton.me**. All reports will be reviewed and handled promptly and fairly. All those responsible for moderation are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter.

Consequences may range from a private warning up to a permanent ban from the project, depending on the severity and recurrence of the behavior.
